01 About

I care about the space between people and technology.

I’m finishing a B.S. in Software Development with a minor in Philosophy at the University of Utah, graduating in December 2026. I combine technical problem-solving with human-centered design: I like asking what a tool should feel like before I decide how it should work.

Lately that has meant leading a WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility audit, designing voice and screen interactions for an embedded device, and building a 3D way to browse books. I’m happiest on teams where design meets genuinely hard engineering.

Running my own drum studio for three and a half years taught me to explain things clearly and to adapt to how different people learn. I bring both to every team I join.
